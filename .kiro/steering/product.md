# Product Overview

Alexis Detailing is a premium mobile car detailing service based in Santiago, Chile. The business operates entirely at customers' locations (domicilio) throughout the Región Metropolitana.

## Core Services
- Lavado Premium (Premium Wash)
- Sellado Cerámico (Ceramic Coating)
- Corrección de Pintura (Paint Correction)
- Pulido (Polishing)
- Descontaminación Clay Bar
- Restauración de Faros (Headlight Restoration)
- Detallado Interior
- Tratamiento de Cuero
- Desinfección con Ozono

## Target Market
- Vehicle owners in Santiago, Chile
- Spanish-speaking audience (es-CL locale)
- Premium service seekers

## Key Value Propositions
- 100% autonomous mobile service (no shop visit required)
- Professional-grade products (Meguiar's, Chemical Guys, Sonax, Gyeon, etc.)
- 6-month warranty on ceramic treatments
- WhatsApp-based booking and communication
